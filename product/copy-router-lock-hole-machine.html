Overview

About this machine

A 1:1 template tracing system duplicates exact shapes without manual marking, while a horizontal three-hole drilling head prepares lock cylinder and handle screw holes at exact centre distances (commonly 21.5 mm apart) in a single stroke.

Key features

  • Template tracing system: 1:1 copying pin with standard template layouts.
  • Multi-head operation: vertical copy milling head plus horizontal three-hole drilling head.
  • Pneumatic clamping: locks profiles in place during high-speed routing.
  • High-speed spindle: up to 12,000 RPM for clean, burr-free cuts in metal and plastic.
  • Adjustable stops & depth control: lateral stops, depth regulators and length stoppers.
  • Simultaneous three-hole drilling: triple-drill mechanism at exact centre distances in one stroke.
  • Dual-axis functionality: vertical lock slot routing plus horizontal handle-hole drilling.
  • Safety integration: two-hand controls, emergency stops, pneumatic interlocks.

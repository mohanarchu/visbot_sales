Overview

About this machine

Built for premium coloured and laminated profiles, the seamless single head welder achieves a tight seam allowance (burn-off) of approximately 0.2 mm, giving a clean appearance that standard welders cannot match.

Key features

  • Weld precision: ~0.2 mm burn-off for a clean appearance on coloured and laminated profiles.
  • Control system: PLC-based automation with digital temperature displays for consistent heating, melting and cooling cycles.
  • Switchable knives: adjustable cutting knives switch easily between normal (2 mm) and seamless (0.2 mm) welding modes.
  • Smooth motion mechanics: linear bearings, hardened steel shafts and LM guide ways for hassle-free heater plate and table movement.
  • Automated PLC & pneumatic systems: pneumatic clamping and transmission for high precision, stable table movement and low vibration.
  • Build quality: high-strength alloy steel frames with anti-corrosion, Teflon-coated heater plates.

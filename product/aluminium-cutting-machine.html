Overview

About this machine

Quick-change swivel tables and fixed locating slots allow fast switching between 90° straight cuts and 45° corner miter joints, with specialised imported 18-inch saw blades engineered for clean, chip-free cutting on both soft plastics (uPVC) and hard alloys (aluminium).

Key features

  • Dual angle adjustments: fast switching between 90° and 45° cuts.
  • Pneumatic clamping: vertical and horizontal air clamps eliminate vibration for burr-free edges.
  • High-precision carbide blades: imported 18-inch blades for uPVC and aluminium.
  • Heavy-duty build: rigid steel / cast iron beds minimise vibration over long runs.
  • Smooth feeding: precision linear bearings for uniform blade movement.
  • Safety guards: protective hoods and shields contain flying chips.
  • Material support racks: adjustable length-stops and roller conveyors for repeatable mass production.

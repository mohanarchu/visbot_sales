Overview

About this machine

The V cutting machine creates the V-shaped grooves needed for the mitered joints of uPVC window and door profiles. Two cutting blades positioned at a 90° angle create the V-groove in a single cycle, producing smooth, chip-free grooves without rough edges.

Key features

  • Pneumatic operation: compressed air drives smooth, easy feeding and clamping.
  • Dual-blade system: two blades at 90° create the V-groove in one cycle.
  • Adjustable settings: V-notch depth adjusts to different profile types.
  • Clean cuts: specialised blades produce smooth, chip-free grooves.
  • High-volume production: quick profile positioning for high-speed fabrication.
  • Efficient material use: minimal cutting waste.
  • Robust construction: built for durability in a fabrication setting.

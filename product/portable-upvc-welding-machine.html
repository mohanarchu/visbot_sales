Overview

About this machine

A portable uPVC welding machine is a compact, lightweight tool designed for on-site fabrication, corner joints and repairs of uPVC window and door profiles. It typically weighs between 21 and 30 kg, runs on standard single-phase electricity (220V/50Hz), and delivers factory-grade joint precision outside the main workshop.

Key features

  • Compact and lightweight design: sturdy aluminium casting or reinforced frame, easy to transport and set up across multi-story or remote construction sites.
  • Digital temperature control: precise digital thermostats and displays monitor and stabilise heating plate temperatures (typically 250–260 °C) for consistent profile fusion.
  • Rapid heating technology: optimised heating elements ensure minimal warm-up times, maximising productivity during busy installation shifts.
  • Teflon-coated heating plates: non-stick coating prevents melted uPVC from adhering, ensuring clean separation and a smooth weld bead.
  • Angle versatility (90° / V-notch / L-shaped): clamps and executes accurate single-point 90-degree corner and joint welds for frames and sashes.
  • Pneumatic or manual clamping: secure clamps hold sections tightly during the melting and joining cycle to eliminate shifting.

Our story

Engineering for India's window makers

From a Coimbatore workshop to shop floors across the country — machinery designed, built and supported by the people who made it.

Founded by an engineer, run like a workshop

Visbot Industries was founded in 2016 by Mr. Ganesh Srinivasan, a mechanical engineer who saw Indian window fabricators paying import prices for machines that were hard to service locally.

Our answer was to develop a full range of uPVC working machinery in-house — cutting, welding, copy routing, finishing and corner cleaning — engineered by skilled professionals using quality-tested components and modern production technology. Every machine is assembled and test-run at our factory on Pollachi Main Road, Eachanari, Coimbatore, before it ships.

Because we design our own machines, we also stand behind them: operator training, on-site installation, and spares that ship from stock rather than from overseas. Today our machines run at major OEMs and window fabricators across India, supported by regional sales contacts in more than eight states and an experience center in Bengaluru where customers can see the machines working before they buy.

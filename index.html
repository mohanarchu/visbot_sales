The process

Four steps from profile to window

One Visbot line covers the complete uPVC fabrication cycle — and we supply every machine in it.

01

Cut

Profiles cut to length and angle on single or double head cutting machines.

02

Weld

Corners fused on welding machines — up to seamless 0.2 mm joints for coloured profiles.

03

Route & Mill

Lock holes, water slots and mullion ends prepared on copy routers and milling machines.

04

Clean & Finish

Weld seams removed with pneumatic and electric corner cleaning tools for a showroom finish.
